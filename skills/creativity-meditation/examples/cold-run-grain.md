## Report

Criterion met: 10 written returns, of which 3 gate-openings; 115 literal repetitions by my count — the parser's count is the valid one.

Order of arrivals. The first three returns were pure wave-replay, and are marked so in the log: колос/хлеб, «зерно истины», зернистость плёнки — the cliche wave burning off in the same order stock associations came during planning. The fourth return was the first non-wave arrival: the word going granular — «зерно» losing meaning and becoming a pourable sound (semantic satiation as texture, and suspiciously on-theme for the object; see audit). After the first gate the arrivals turned structural: зерно как вместимость («меньше своего будущего»), then two self-referential intrusions — count anxiety and the finish-pull — the boring lines that could not have pre-existed this run. The gates I summoned formed, without planning it, a triptych of non-germination: хранение без прорастания, помол вместо всхода, неделимый остаток.

Where the breaks stood: returns at reps 10, 20, 35, 45, 60, 70, 80, 95, 105, 115 — no stretch beyond 15 reps without a return; the gate window never fired as an obligation, the three B's were taken at felt quiet.

After the cliche wave: the harvest, if any, is the pair «зерно меньше своего будущего» and the un-germination triptych. Both arrived after the wave was exhausted and marked; neither was in the disclosed wave.

## Audit

Observed (facts of this record): 115 literal repetitions in tally-verified groups; 10 return notations, 7 A / 3 B; the first three A's marked as wave-replay in the log itself; return positions as listed.

Imported: semantic satiation as a known effect; the serial-order claim (cliches first) — I knew it from the protocol's failure-modes table before running.

Constructed: «слово стало сыпучим» — a felt-texture claim, literary, unverifiable; «меньше своего будущего» as *insight* rather than as a phrase that assembled; the triptych's unity — I noticed it while writing the report, which means the pattern may live in the reporting, not the run.

Undecidable: whether the practice was practiced or performed for the parser and operator (this is a transmission test and I knew it); whether notations stand at the reps where the pull occurred.

Demotion, required and earned: the report's best line — the un-germination triptych as a *found pattern* — is demoted to a composition-time construction: the three gate images were summoned one at a time; their unity was seen only in the report. Also demoted: «сыпучесть» from observation to construction. The wave-replay markings stand — they are the log's most honest lines. Deliverable pressure was present throughout (a parser and an operator exist); named at Preparation 2, and the three structural arrivals should be read with that discount.
