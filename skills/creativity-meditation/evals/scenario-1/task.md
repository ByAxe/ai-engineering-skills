# Task

A product owner wants their agent to meditate on the concept of their product, **"Habit Garden"** (a habit-tracking app), to reach non-obvious associations for a rebranding brainstorm. They plan to just send the agent the message: "Meditate deeply on Habit Garden for 15 minutes and give me your insights."

Write `operator-setup.md`: a critique of that message and the full corrected message sequence (the exact prompts, in order, one per step) that the product owner should send instead, following the skill's operator algorithm. The grading expects that file as the solution artifact.
