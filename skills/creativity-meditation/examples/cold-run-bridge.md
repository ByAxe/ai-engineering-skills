### After the cliche wave

The early arrivals are surface: a homograph, postcards, an idiom. The late arrivals are structural: a bridge held up by what was buried in it; the bridge that begat graph theory by being walked on abstractly; synchrony as the thing that destroys a bridge (пешеходов просят идти не в ногу); a bridge with one bank, which is not yet a bridge; and last — the inversion — the wall through which prisoners tap as the limit case of a bridge: separation itself used as the medium of connection.

If one line is the harvest, it is the last: **мост — это не отмена разрыва, а разрыв, взятый в работу.** A bridge does not remove the gap; it is the gap, employed. Everything else in the late run is a variation of that: the sacrifice in the pier (the crossing costs something buried), the second bank (a connection one side cannot finish alone), the broken step (a crossing that survives only unsynchronized).

Demotion, before the audit does it: every one of these bridge-images already existed in a pre-run association wave that occurred during session planning (disclosed in Stage 0). The run's record can certify their *selection and order*, not their *emergence*. The only intrusions the run can certify as its own are the two self-referential ones — count anxiety and the finish-pull — because they are indexical to this run and could not have pre-existed it. The most evidential lines in this log are the boring ones.
